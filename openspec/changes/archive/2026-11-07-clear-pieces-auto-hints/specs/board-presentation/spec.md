## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Prominent king
The original SVG piece set SHALL use classic distinct silhouettes. The king SHALL have a large cross, a broad body, and greater visual height than the queen, bishop, rook, knight, and pawn, while fitting its square. The same designs SHALL appear in board, replay, miniature, and promotion views.

#### Scenario: Compact board
- **WHEN** the starting board is shown at 320 by 568 pixels
- **THEN** both kings are distinct from the queens and bishops without relying on color or labels
